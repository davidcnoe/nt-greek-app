## THE LAST WORDS OF SOCRATES TO HIS JUDGES.

These selections may fitly close with one of the noblest and yet easiest passages in Classical literature. Socrates was condemned to death by the Athenians on the charge of corrupting the youth and of introducing the worship of strange gods. The passage below consists of part of his address to the judges who voted for his acquittal.
