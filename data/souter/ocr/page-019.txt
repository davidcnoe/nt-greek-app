ἀλαλάξζω

ἀλαλάζω (onomatopoeic, cf. Hebrew), 7 cry aloud,
generally of persons (in Mk. v 38 from sorrow):
κύμβαλον -ἀζον, a clanging or clashing cymbal (1 Cor.
xiii 1).

ἀλάλητος, unullerable, that baffles words.

ἄλαλος, dumb.

ἅλας (neut.), salt.

ἄλεεύς (a modification of the earlier ἁλιεύς), a fisher-
man.

ἀλείφω, I anoint.

ἀλεκτοροφωνία (galli cantus, gallicinium), cockerow, as
a period of time, between midnight and 3 a.m.

ἀλέκτωρ, a cock.

᾿Αλεξανδρεύς, an Alexandrian, a native (or resident) of
Alexandria in Egypt.

᾿Αλεξανδρινός (or -wos), lelonging lo Alexandria in
Egypt.

᾿Αλέξανδρος, Alexander, a proper name of Greek origin,
borne by four, possibly five, persons in the N.T.,
(a) an early Christian, son of Simon of Cyrene, who
carried the Cross, Mk, xv zr; (0) a leading non-
Christian Jew in Jerusalem, Ac.iv 6; (0) an
Ephesian Jew, Ac. xix 33; (d) a renegade Christian
at Rome {ε Tim.izo), probably to be identified
with Alexander the coppersmith (2 Tim. iv 14).

ἄλευρον, meal.

ἀλήθεια, fruih, but not merely truth as spoken; truth
of idea, reality, sincerity, truth in the moral sphere,
straightforwardness : ἐπ᾽ ἀληθείας, really, truly.

ἀληθεύω, 7 say (speak) truth, Gal.ivi6; 1 do frufl,
1 maintain iruih (the truth) (see ἀλήθεια for the sense
of “truth”).

ἀληθής, rue in fact; hence more widely (see ἀλήθεια),

ἀληθινός (less common than ἀληθής), frue (lit. made of
truth), real, genuine.

ἀλήθω, 7 grind.

ἀληθῶς, ἡμῖν, verily,

ἁλιεύς, see deeds.
