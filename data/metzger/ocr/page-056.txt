B. Suffixes Forming Adjectives

1. Adjectives cxpressing the meaning of or belonging to
a person or thing are formed by adding the suffix =10¢ to a
noun-stem.

Exanples: οὐράν-τιος, heavenly (from οὐρανός, heaven)

2. Adjectives expressing the idea belonging to, pertain-
ing to, with the characteristics of, are formed by adding the
suffix τίχος to a noun-stem.

Examples: βασιλ-εικός, belonging to a king, kingly,

royal (from βασιλεύς, a kina)
