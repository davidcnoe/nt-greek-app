A few observations as to the most efficient ways in which
to use the following word lists may not be out of place. The
usual and time-tested procedure is to concentrate on a Greek
word and to repeat it to oneself over and over again with the
English definition. In doing so one should be careful always
to put the stress on the syllable of the Greek word which car-
ries the accent mark. Otherwise, if, for instance, ἀδελφός be
nronounced a’del-fos today, and a-del’fos tomorrow, and a-del-
fos” at another time, the labor of learning the one Greek word
is practically tripled.
